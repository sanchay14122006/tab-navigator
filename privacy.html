Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Tab Navigator handles information when you use the Google Sheets add-on.

Information and spreadsheet access

Tab Navigator requests Google Sheets access to provide its spreadsheet navigation and management features. Depending on the feature you use, it may read sheet names, sheet properties, and cell contents, and may make changes you request, such as hiding, showing, recoloring, or organizing sheets.

Searches are performed to provide the feature you invoke. Do not use the add-on with information you are not authorized to access.

Storage and sharing

The add-on stores user-specific preferences and organizational settings using Google Apps Script's user properties. These settings are associated with your Google account and are used to provide your preferences.

We do not operate a separate server or database to collect or store your spreadsheet contents. This statement must be updated if the app later introduces external analytics, telemetry, or other data transmission.

Google services

The add-on runs using Google services and is subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices. Google may process information as necessary to provide those services.

Data retention and deletion

Preferences may remain stored in your Google Apps Script user properties until they are changed or deleted. You can stop using the add-on or revoke its Google account access through your Google account settings.

Children

Tab Navigator is a general productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the product changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
